Depth Guides

UnityC#WebGL3D interaction

Question: Do simple visual depth cues help people place objects more accurately in 3D?

What I built: A mini experiment where you move a cube to match a target in 3D. Press G to switch on depth guides, a vertical line and a floor "shadow" under each object. It records placement error and time, and compares guides on and off.

What I learned: How much 3D interaction depends on judging depth, and how one small visual cue changes what people can see. Inspired by research on non-intrusive visual guides for 3D drawing.

AR

Mobile AR starter app

UnityAR FoundationAndroid

What I built: An augmented reality app built with Unity's AR Foundation and deployed to an Android phone. It checks whether the device supports AR, shows a live on-screen log of the AR session, and renders a 3D object.

What I learned: The full pipeline from a Unity scene to an app on a real phone, and how to debug an AR session that won't start (the device needed Google Play Services for AR). I wrote the C# with help from AI tools and am now learning to write and debug it on my own.
